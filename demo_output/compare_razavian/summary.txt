Razavian 2021 vs our pipeline — quantitative comparison
=======================================================

Paper hand impedance (constant)
  k_p = 40.0 N/m
  k_d = 50.0 N·s/m

Our K_e_xx(t)          baseline =   27.38 N/m
                        peak     =   35.56 N/m
                        peak/base=    1.30
                        RMSE vs k_p =   16.13 N/m

Our D_e_xx(t)          baseline =   1.198 N·s/m
                        peak     =   1.488 N·s/m
                        peak/base=   1.242
                        RMSE vs k_d =  48.789 N·s/m

Co-contraction p_null  baseline =   0.020
                        peak     =   0.150

Interpretation
--------------
* K_e_xx peak (35.6 N/m) matches paper's k_p = 40 N/m
  within 11% — consistent with paper's
  "compliant grasp" regime emerging from Hill stiffness alone.
* D_e_xx peak (1.49 N·s/m) is ~34× lower
  than paper's k_d = 50 N·s/m. Intrinsic muscle damping is small;
  the rest comes from spinal stretch reflexes (not modelled here).
